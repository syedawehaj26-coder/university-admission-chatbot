Question: What are the eligibility criteria for undergraduate programs?
Keywords: eligibility, eligible, criteria, requirement, requirements, minimum marks, percentage, qualification, qualify, fsc, intermediate, a level, o level, ibcc, equivalence, grades
Answer: Engineering programs need at least 60% in F.Sc (Pre-Engineering) or equivalent. All other programs need at least 50% in F.Sc or equivalent. O/A-Level and foreign qualifications need an IBCC equivalence certificate. The minimum number of passed subjects is 8 for O Level and 3 for A Level.

Question: What documents are required for admission?
Keywords: documents, document, certificates, certificate, required, submit, cnic, form-b, marksheet, mark sheet, papers, bring, original, affidavit, attested
Answer: You need your CNIC or Form-B; SSC certificate (or O-Level IBCC equivalence) and SSC marks sheet (or O-Level GCE); HSSC certificate (or A-Level IBCC equivalence) and HSSC Part-I and Part-II marks sheets (or A-Level GCE AS and A2); a Statement of Entry if A-Level results are awaited; HEC equivalence for foreign degrees; and an affidavit on plain paper if any result or equivalence is awaited. Print and fill the provisional admission form and bring it with all original documents on interview day.

Question: Is there an entry test?
Keywords: entry test, admission test, test, buet, syllabus, mcq, cbt, computer based test, test pattern, negative marking, sample paper, mock paper, subjects
Answer: Yes. Applicants sit the Bahria University Entry Test (BUET), a computer-based test of 100 MCQs in 120 minutes. There is no negative marking for undergraduate tests. Engineering and computing programs: 30% Verbal Ability, 15% Quantitative Reasoning, 15% Analytical Reasoning, 10% Physics and 30% Maths. Earth and Environmental Sciences: 25% each of Verbal, Quantitative, Analytical, and Biology or Maths plus Chemistry and Physics. BS Psychology: 50% English, 25% General Knowledge and 25% Verbal Reasoning. Syllabus page: https://www.bahria.edu.pk/page/PageTemplate4?pageContentId=4663&WebsiteID=1

Question: How do I apply?
Keywords: apply, application, procedure, process, form, admission form, online, portal, register, registration, submit form, steps, link, how to apply
Answer: Apply online at https://cms.bahria.edu.pk/Logins/Candidate/Login.aspx. Create an account, set your password from the email link, fill in the form and generate a fee voucher, pay the admission processing fee at any Alfalah or Allied Bank branch, print your entry test slip, appear in the BUET, check the merit list on www.bahria.edu.pk, attend your interview, and then pay the first semester fee.

Question: How is merit decided and is there an interview?
Keywords: merit, merit list, interview, selection, shortlisted, selected, select, choose, how are students admitted
Answer: Merit is announced on the basis of the entry test result, your academic qualification marks (intermediate or equivalent) and the interview. Shortlisted candidates are called for an interview, so keep your original documents ready. Seats are limited, so admission depends on merit and the number of available seats.

Question: What is the fee refund policy?
Keywords: refund, refunds, money back, return fee, withdraw, withdrawal, cancel, cancellation
Answer: A full (100%) refund is available up to the 7th day of the commencement of classes for the semester system, except the admission fee, which is non-refundable. Keywords: about, bahria, navy, established, founded, history, introduction, what is bahria
